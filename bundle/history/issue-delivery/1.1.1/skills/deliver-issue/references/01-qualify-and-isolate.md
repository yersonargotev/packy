# Qualify and isolate

Fetch the named issue, labels, comments, dependencies, and referenced
normative material through `gh`. Confirm the policy's exact approval condition,
one clear objective, verifiable acceptance criteria, explicit limits, and
sufficient dependency and prerequisite state. Confirm that no existing open
pull request or active branch already delivers the issue.

Read every repository instruction and normative source required by policy,
including affected specialist-surface policies. For a reported bug with an
uncertain cause or reproduction, diagnose it before planning the change. Keep
the approved objective, criteria, limits, and authority intact; facts may be
collected, but intent is never rewritten silently.

Fetch the protected base branch, record the operator checkout, and choose the
isolated workspace under the common invariants. Record the exact qualified
starting commit. If policy, authority, scope, dependencies, or ownership is
ambiguous, issue the exception brief before implementation.

**Complete when:** the exact issue authority, implementable acceptance surface,
dependencies, governing contracts, isolated workspace, and starting commit are
known, with no competing delivery.
